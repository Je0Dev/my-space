---
title: On slow mornings
date: 2026-10-08
tags: [thoughts]
---

There's a particular kind of morning where nothing is planned and the day is entirely unwritten. No meetings, no notifications, just coffee and a window.

I used to fill those mornings immediately — phone, news, other people's opinions. Lately I've been leaving them empty on purpose. It turns out the quiet is where most of my actual thinking happens.

A small ritual that helps:

1. Coffee first, phone later.
2. One page of writing, even if it's bad.
3. A walk without headphones.

Slow is not the same as unproductive. Sometimes it's the opposite.
