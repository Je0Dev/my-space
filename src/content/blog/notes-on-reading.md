I read a lot as a kid, less as an adult. Trying to reverse that.

A few things that have worked:

- **Always carry a book.** Not a huge revelation, but the book on the nightstand gets read; the one on the shelf doesn't.
- **Quit books freely.** Life is too short for books that aren't holding your attention.
- **Reread the good ones.** Some books are better the second time around.
- **Write a line or two after finishing.** Forces you to actually absorb instead of just consume.

Current stack on the nightstand: a novel, a book of essays, and one I'll probably never finish. That's fine too.
